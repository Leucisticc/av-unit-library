What this is

Every Anime Vanguards unit and memoria with the full text of its passives and active abilities, pulled from the wiki. Switch between Units and Memoria with the tabs; filters work the same on both. A memoria's first passive works on anyone; its Exclusive passives only work on the unit(s) or element(s) shown. Each passive is tagged with the status effects it applies and the buffs it gives, so you can find units by what they do instead of guessing keywords.

Filters

  • Damage over Time, Crowd Control, Damage Amplification, Other Effects — every status effect on the wiki's Status Effects page, in its groups. Tick an effect to show units that apply it. Unit-specific effects (Stone, Despair, Infinite Spin…) also carry the general effect they do (Stun, Slow, Repulse…). Units that only react to it ("deals more damage to Bleeding enemies") are not included — look under Mechanics → Conditional Bonus Damage for those.
  • Buffs — what the unit boosts. Buffs self and Buffs allies say who gets it. Hover Buffs allies or Buffs self for a side panel that narrows to an element, a group, or a specific unit — type its name and you get every unit whose buff reaches it (all-ally buffs, its element, its groups, or buffs that name it). Buffs whose target the wiki text doesn't state are listed under "Target not stated" and never match a picked unit. For Buffs self the panel asks which unit is affected: a memoria's universal passive affects anyone wearing it, an Exclusive or conditional one (Cosmic units only…) affects only those; a unit's own self-buff affects only that unit. To find memoria a unit can simply wear, exclude Exclusive instead.
  • Mechanics — summons, meters, stacks, ignoring damage reduction, anti-shield, hits-everything.
  • Element, Rarity, State, Group — the unit itself. State is Unevolved (has an evolution), Evolved, or No evolution.

Ticking several boxes in one dropdown means any of them. Using several dropdowns means all of them. Click ✕ on a row to exclude that effect instead.

Search box

Plain words search unit names and passive / ability text (not trivia). The filters write short codes into the same box, so you can also type them:

  • dot:bleed applies Bleed · dot:bleed+burn Bleed or Burn · dot:bleed dot:burn Bleed and Burn
  • buffs:ally-buff bufftarget:fire buffs Fire allies · buffsunit:sukono buffs that reach Sukono · cc:repulse -element:fire repulses, not Fire
  • Codes: dot cc amp effect buffs mech bufftarget buffsunit element rarity state group; tag:x matches any group; - in front excludes.

Reading a unit

Click a unit to see all its passives, familiar passives and actives. While a filter is on, the exact words that earned the tag are underlined in that group's colour, so you can check the tag yourself. Tag pills on a card are clickable — they add that filter.
